Which public funds quietly hold private companies?

13Forage assembles high-level equity research on institutional managers with reported exposure to late-stage private companies — Anthropic, Anduril, OpenAI, SpaceX and their peers — by pairing curated analysis with live SEC filing data.

Read this first. A 13F filing discloses only 13(f) securities — US-listed equities, ADRs, and some options. A fund's direct stake in a private company does not appear in its 13F. Everything tagged as private exposure below is reconstructed from public reporting on listed vehicles, trusts, and holding companies — not read off a holdings table. Marked reported where unverified.
